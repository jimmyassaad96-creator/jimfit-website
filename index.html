A little clarity

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Will JimFit be on Google Play and the App Store?

Yes — JimFit is coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

Does AI replace my trainer?

No. JimFit’s diet plans and training programs are created by professionals. AI supports tasks such as voice entry and understanding your training information. Your trainer provides personal guidance and makes the coaching decisions.

Can I use JimFit without a trainer?

Yes. JimFit is for independent trainees, members working with a coach, and trainers managing clients online or in person.

How does the member trial work?

Members can explore JimFit for 14 days with no card required. Contact us for membership options after your trial.

What does the trainer plan include?

Every feature, on both plans: client management, training programs, live session logging, voice logging in Lebanese and Arabic, AI chat, analytics, scheduling, attendance and packages. Starter is $12.99 per month for up to 5 clients. Pro is $24.99 per month for 6 or more. Trainers get 14 days free once their account is approved.

How is my trainer plan chosen?

By your client count. Up to 5 clients is Starter. From 6, you move to Pro at your next payment, and back to Starter if you drop to 5. Your own training log doesn’t count, and you’re never blocked from adding a client.

Are diet plans included?

Diet tracking and diet plans are separate offerings. Diet plans are created by professionals and available for a one-time $30 payment. Check the available options in the app.

How can I pay?

Pay by Whish Money at 71 107 437, by neo, or in cash. With Whish, include the email used for your JimFit account. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm your payment.

Is Google Play payment available?

Not yet. For now, pay by Whish Money, neo or cash.
